### What was examined and decided NOT to be a defect

1. **Identifying employees by `emp_code` instead of MongoDB `_id`**:
   The problem statement and `DATA_MODEL.md` explicitly state that `emp_code` is a customer-assigned business identifier (`EMP0001`), unique and immutable. MongoDB `_id` is an internal implementation detail and must never appear in API requests or responses.
2. **Storing dates as strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and shift times as strings (`HH:MM`)**:
   Section 1 of `DATA_MODEL.md` notes that calendar dates and times of day are plain strings on purpose, because storing them as BSON dates would attach an artificial timestamp and timezone.
3. **No client authentication / authorization headers**:
   The problem statement explicitly clarifies that no authentication or permission system is expected for this assignment; `regularized_by` in corrections is simply captured as a plain string.
